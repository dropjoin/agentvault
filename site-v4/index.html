STEP 1

Owner sets the policy

The vault owner fixes the rules on-chain: a per-transaction cap, a daily cap, and the list of recipients the agent is allowed to pay.

STEP 2

Agent spends inside the caps

The AI agent holds its own key and pays for services from the vault. Any payment outside the policy reverts on-chain, no exceptions.

STEP 3

Every move is auditable

Deposits, spends and policy changes are recorded as events on Arbitrum Sepolia, so the owner always sees the same truth as the chain.
