The demo that proves it

The demo agent tried two jobs. Job 1 paid the allowlisted service 0.01 ETH — inside policy, executed on-chain (tx on Arbiscan). Job 2 attempted 0.5 ETH — the vault rejected it on-chain with per-tx cap exceeded, even though the agent key was valid and the vault held the funds. That rejection is the product.
